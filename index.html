About Our School

🎯

Our Mission

To provide quality, inclusive, and accessible education that equips students with critical thinking, ethical values, and 21st-century skills for national development.

🌟

Our Vision

To be the leading educational institution in Nuwakot, producing responsible citizens, compassionate leaders, and skilled professionals who contribute to Nepal's progress.

💎

Our Values

Integrity, discipline, respect for diversity, academic excellence, and community service are the cornerstones of our school culture and daily practice.

Academic Programs

📖

Primary Level (Grade 1–5)

A nurturing foundation in core subjects — Nepali, English, Mathematics, Science, and Social Studies — with emphasis on learning through play and exploration.

🔬

Lower Secondary (Grade 6–8)

Deeper engagement with science, mathematics, and humanities. Introduction to computer education and practical life skills to prepare well-rounded students.

🎓

Secondary Level (Grade 9–12)

Rigorous SEE and +2 preparation with Science, Management, and Humanities streams. Strong focus on results, university readiness, and career guidance.
